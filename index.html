A transparent, rule-based estimate that combines the Open-Meteo forecast with recent community reports. Open "Why this prediction?" to see every factor and how many points it adds.

How the scoring model works

Each risk starts at 0% and adds points for forecast conditions (rain in the next 24 h and 72 h, rain probability, maximum and minimum temperature, wind, thunderstorms) and for unresolved community reports from the last 72 hours in your area. Higher-severity reports add more points (Low +0, Medium +3, High +6, Critical +9 on top of a base). Reports that show help is available — tankers, solar charging, lift offers, food parcels, produce surplus — lower the risk. Some risks carry over: transport and energy disruption raise food-access risk. Scores are capped at 100%. Bands: 0–24 Low, 25–49 Moderate, 50–74 High, 75+ Severe. This is a guide, not an official warning — always follow SAWS and municipal notices.

Conservation hub

Water & energy saving guide

Practical ways to save water and electricity. No login needed. Track the ones you've done in My Resilience Plan.

Household

Fix leaking taps and toilets immediately

Replace worn washers where safe and report leaks you can't repair to your municipality. A dripping tap can waste 20 litres a day.

Take shorter showers with a low-flow shower head

Keep showers brief and turn water off while soaping. Less hot water also means a lower electricity bill.

Only run full loads in washing machines

Use full loads and an eco cycle when available.

Garden

Water in the early morning or late evening

Water close to the soil when it's cool to reduce evaporation.

Mulch garden beds to keep moisture in the soil

Dry leaves, compost or straw shade soil and slow moisture loss.

Install rainwater tanks

Use covered tanks and keep harvested rainwater separate from drinking water unless properly treated.

Energy & preparedness

Shift appliance use off-peak

Run geysers, pool pumps, washing machines and dishwashers outside 06:00–09:00 and 17:00–21:00 where possible. It eases pressure on the grid and can lower time-of-use bills.

Charge devices before expected disruptions

Keep phones, power banks and rechargeable lights charged when load-shedding or storms are forecast.

Prepare a household emergency kit

Stored water (10–20 litres per person), torch, power bank, first-aid kit, medication, copies of ID documents, non-perishable food and a list of emergency numbers.

Legal · Your data

POPIA Notice

Mati-Metsi complies with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). This section explains what we collect and your rights.

What we collect

  • Your name and email — only when you register or sign in. Your name is shown next to your reports and help listings.
  • Approximate location — the city/suburb you type for weather, your home area and the suburb on reports and listings. The weather place name is sent to Open-Meteo to fetch the forecast.
  • Reports, photos and help listings you submit — content you voluntarily provide. Photos are resized and stored on this device.
  • Optional contact details on help listings — a phone or WhatsApp number only if you choose to add one; it is shown to signed-in members only.
  • Preferences — alert subscriptions, thresholds, resilience-plan progress, language and display settings.
  • Session data — a browser token in localStorage that remembers you're signed in.

What we don't collect

  • Precise GPS location — the app never asks for it
  • Exact home addresses — the app warns you if a suburb field looks like a street address
  • Browsing history or tracking cookies
  • Third-party advertising identifiers
  • Financial or biometric data

How we protect your data (POPIA Section 19)

  • HTTPS/TLS on all connections when hosted on HTTPS.
  • Passwords are hashed with SHA-256 in your browser — never stored in plain text.
  • No third-party trackers (no Google Analytics, no ad networks).
  • Your session, reports, listings, alerts and plan live in your browser's localStorage on this device — they are not sent to our servers. Other people only see them if they use the same device. Report photos are stored separately (mm_photos) so a future backend sync can never lose them. Resolved reports older than 12 months are deleted automatically when the app opens.
  • Browser notifications are optional and only shown if you grant permission.

Your rights (POPIA Chapter 5)

  1. Access — download your data anytime from your profile.
  2. Deletion — delete your account and all your data with one click.
  3. Correction — edit your profile.
  4. Complaint — lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

About · Roadmap · Business model

About Mati-Metsi

Mati-Metsi is a South African community resilience platform for water, energy, transport and food — built to help households and neighbourhoods prepare, coordinate and recover together.

The problem

South African households face overlapping, recurring disruptions: water outages and leaks, load-shedding and grid faults, flooded and closed roads, and food-price spikes. These hit the same people on the same days — but today coordination happens in scattered WhatsApp groups and Facebook posts, with no memory, no verification and no bridge to formal municipal or NGO response.

  • Municipalities lose an estimated 25–40% of treated water before it reaches homes (DWS estimates).
  • Load-shedding has repeatedly disrupted water pumping, food cold-chains and traffic lights.
  • Food-price inflation has hit low-income households hardest, compounding the effect of every other outage.

Why Mati-Metsi

  • One coordination layer across all four essential services — not four separate apps.
  • Transparent predictions — every risk and every personal insight shows its working ("Why this prediction?" / "How we know").
  • Verified-partner bridge — municipal and NGO responders can confirm reports, update status and post public response notes next to community reports.
  • All 11 official South African languages — inclusion is built in, not bolted on.
  • Offline-first and POPIA-safe — everything works on the device, with no GPS, no trackers and no third parties.

Impact context

  • Water, energy, transport and food are the four services households cannot do without.
  • When they fail, the poorest households lose the most income and the most time.
  • Small, well-organised community signals (reports, confirmations, offers of help) measurably shorten recovery.
  • Mati-Metsi turns scattered community knowledge into structured, shareable, actionable information.

Independent figures for specific metros are available on request.

Pilot & traction plan

We are preparing an 8-week pilot in a single ward in Tshwane. The pilot is designed to answer three questions with real users:

  1. Will households report and confirm? Target: 100 reports/week with ≥ 1 confirmation per report.
  2. Will neighbours help each other? Target: ≥ 20 active help-board listings with ≥ 50% completion.
  3. Will readiness improve? Target: average household readiness score up by 25 points.

Partners, letters of intent and pilot results will be published here as they are confirmed.

Roadmap

  1. Phase 1 — this build. Single-file PWA, all 11 official languages, offline-first, demo data, personal insights, verified-partner workflow.
  2. Phase 2 — shared backend (not yet shipped). Supabase/Firebase so reports are shared across devices; magic-link login; per-ward moderation.
  3. Phase 3 — reach (service-worker file provided for download, not yet registered; WhatsApp/SMS not yet connected). Push notifications via a service worker; WhatsApp Business API; SMS fallback for feature phones.
  4. Phase 4 — formal integration (partner dashboard shipped; official feeds pending). Official SAWS and municipal feeds; verified-partner dashboard; public data export.
  5. Phase 5 — insights. Anonymised, POPIA-safe aggregate dashboards for planners and insurers.

Business model

  • Municipal SaaS — licence per ward per month, with a free community tier.
  • NGO and grant funding — community tier funded by development partners.
  • Verified-partner listing — optional low-fee verified badge for partner organisations.
  • Anonymised aggregate insights — non-personal, POPIA-compliant dashboards for planners and insurers.
  • No advertising, no trackers, no data sales.
