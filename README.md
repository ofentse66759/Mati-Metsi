# Mati-Metsi — Township Resilience OS

A community resilience platform for South African townships. Mati-Metsi helps households prepare for water, energy, transport and food disruptions with live weather, community reports, neighbour help, and a personalised Resilience Plan.

## What's in this build

- ✅ All 11 official South African languages
- ✅ Live weather via WeatherAPI.com and Open-Meteo
- ✅ Transparent rule-based risk model (water, flood, heat, transport, food)
- ✅ Community reports with a 4-stage lifecycle and 3-confirmation promotion
- ✅ Verified-partner dashboard (demo accounts only)
- ✅ Neighbour Help board with address and banking guards
- ✅ Resilience Plan with Rand, kWh and litre savings
- ✅ POPIA export and one-click account deletion
- ✅ Offline outbox with auto-send on reconnect
- ✅ Service worker for offline caching and push notifications

## Roadmap (not yet shipped)

- Shared backend (Supabase / Firebase)
- WhatsApp Business API and SMS fallback
- SAWS rainfall and Rand Water reservoir feeds
- Leaflet map of verified JoJo tanks and boreholes
